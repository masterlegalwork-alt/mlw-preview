Consent, and how to withdraw it

Each form asks for consent separately for each purpose, and no box is ticked in advance. The weekly email is always optional and is never a condition of booking.

You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to masterlegalwork@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect anything done before it; we then stop the processing, unless the law requires a record to be kept.

Who processes the data for us

  • Google LLC: Gmail, Google Sheets (“MLW Website Leads”), Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Meet and Apps Script: email, record-keeping, document folders, hearing reminders and video calls.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): Delivers website form submissions to masterlegalwork@gmail.com (until the Google Apps Script form handler is in use).
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): Messages and calls you choose to have with the chambers on WhatsApp.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): Hosts this website; its servers record technical access logs such as IP addresses.
  • Your bank or UPI app, and the chambers’ bank: Process any payment you make.

Some of these services store data on servers outside India, as permitted by law. We do not sell or share personal data for marketing.

Your rights

  • to a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • to correct, complete or update it;
  • to have it erased when it is no longer needed for the purpose, unless the law requires it to be kept;
  • to nominate another person to exercise these rights in the event of your death or incapacity;
  • to grievance redressal, as below.

Grievances, and complaints to the Data Protection Board

Grievance contact: Advocate Gagandeep Goel, masterlegalwork@gmail.com, +91 9872206969. We will acknowledge your request and respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with the response, or receive none within that time, you have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India, which accepts complaints online, after you have used our grievance process.

Other points

  • Children: we do not knowingly collect a child’s data without the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian.
  • Browser storage: the site stores one item in your browser (localStorage) to remember that you accepted the entry disclaimer, and may keep recently viewed pages for offline use. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Security: please do not upload case documents to this website; share them only through a Google Drive folder as described on the booking page.
  • Data breaches: if a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
