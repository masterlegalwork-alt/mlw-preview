Updates

What changed in the law

A dated record of changes in the law and in court practice that matter for litigants in Chandigarh, Punjab and Haryana, each with its source.

  1. 02 June 2026

    P&H High Court practice

    P&H High Court: revised objections checklist for civil writs

    The High Court published its objections checklist codes for civil writ petitions as on 2 June 2026.

    Source: P&H High Court, Objection Check List (Writ)

  2. 23 January 2026

    P&H High Court practice

    P&H High Court: notice on e-filing

    The High Court issued a notice regarding e-filing dated 23 January 2026.

    Source: P&H High Court, Notifications & Guidelines

  3. 17 March 2025

    P&H High Court practice

    P&H High Court: e-filing note

    A further note on e-filing took effect from 17 March 2025.

    Source: P&H High Court, Notifications & Guidelines

  4. 07 January 2025

    P&H High Court practice

    P&H High Court: e-filing guidelines

    Guidelines for e-filing of cases in the Punjab & Haryana High Court took effect from 7 January 2025.

    Source: P&H High Court, Notifications & Guidelines

  5. 01 July 2024

    BNS / BNSS / BSA

    New criminal laws in force

    The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 came into force, replacing the IPC, the CrPC and the Indian Evidence Act.

    Source: NCRB correspondence tables

  6. 14 May 2024

    Consumer Protection Act, 2019

    Advocates' services held outside the Consumer Protection Act

    The Supreme Court held that a complaint alleging deficiency in service against an advocate is not maintainable under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Bar of Indian Lawyers v. D.K. Gandhi, 2024 INSC 410).

    Source: Judgment (Indian Kanoon)

Each entry checked against its source on 10 October 2026.
