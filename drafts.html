Respectfully showeth:

  7. That the cause of action arose on [DATE] when [the goods were supplied / the amount was advanced], on [DATE] when payment fell due, on [DATE] when the legal notice was served, and it is continuing as the amount remains unpaid.
  8. That this Court has territorial jurisdiction as [the defendant resides / carries on business / the cause of action, wholly or in part, arose] at [PLACE], within the jurisdiction of this Court. The suit is within the pecuniary jurisdiction of this Court.
  9. That the suit is within limitation, being filed within three years from [DATE OF DUE DATE / LAST PAYMENT / ACKNOWLEDGMENT] [Article __ of the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963].
  10. That the suit is valued at ₹[AMOUNT] for the purposes of court fee and jurisdiction, and the requisite court fee of ₹[__] has been affixed.
  11. That no other suit or proceeding on the same cause of action is pending or has been decided between the parties.

Respectfully showeth:

  1. That the complainant and the accused are known to each other [STATE RELATIONSHIP / BUSINESS DEALINGS].
  2. That the accused owed a legally enforceable debt / liability of ₹[AMOUNT] to the complainant on account of [LOAN / SALE OF GOODS / SERVICES / OTHER — brief facts].
  3. That in discharge of the said liability, the accused issued cheque No. [__] dated [DATE] for ₹[AMOUNT], drawn on [BANK, BRANCH] (Annexure C-1).

[NAME OF COMPLAINANT], [S/o / D/o / W/o] [NAME OF FATHER / HUSBAND], aged about [__] years, resident of [FULL ADDRESS WITH PIN CODE]… Respondent No. 2

Respectfully showeth:

  1. That the above-noted FIR was registered at the instance of respondent No. 2 against the petitioner(s), alleging that [BRIEF ALLEGATIONS].
  2. That the dispute between the parties is [private / personal / matrimonial / commercial] in nature.
  3. That with the intervention of respectable persons and well-wishers, the parties have amicably settled their disputes and entered into a written compromise dated [DATE] (Annexure P-2). The compromise is voluntary and without any coercion, pressure or undue influence.
  4. That respondent No. 2 has no objection to the quashing of the FIR and the consequential proceedings, and is ready to appear before this Hon'ble Court or before the trial court to make a statement to that effect.
  5. That the proceedings are at the stage of [INVESTIGATION / CHARGE / EVIDENCE] before [COURT]. In view of the compromise, the chances of conviction are remote, and continuing the proceedings would be an abuse of the process of the court.
  6. That the petitioner(s) [have / have not] been declared proclaimed offender(s), and [no other case is pending between the parties / details of other cases: __].
