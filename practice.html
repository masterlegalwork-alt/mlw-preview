Areas of practice

Areas of practice

The kinds of matters handled by the chamber. This is general information only. It is not legal advice and makes no promise about any outcome.

List of practice areas

  • 01

    Civil litigation

    Civil suits, injunctions, recovery, declaration and partition matters, and related applications and appeals.

  • 02

    Criminal matters and bail

    Regular and anticipatory bail, trials, revisions, appeals and petitions for quashing.

  • 03

    Matrimonial matters

    Divorce, maintenance, custody and guardianship, and domestic violence proceedings.

  • 04

    Cheque dishonour

    Complaints and defence under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.

  • 05

    Consumer disputes

    Complaints, replies and appeals before the Consumer Commissions.

  • 06

    Arbitration

    Arbitration proceedings and related court applications under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

  • 07

    Writ matters

    Writ petitions under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution before the Punjab & Haryana High Court.

  • 08

    Landlord and tenant

    Rent, tenancy and eviction matters.

  • 09

    Service and banking recovery

    Service matters before the Central Administrative Tribunal and recovery matters before the Debts Recovery Tribunal.

  • 10

    Motor accident claims

    Claim petitions and defence before the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal.

  • 11

    Legal notices and drafting

    Legal notices and replies, settlement deeds, and drafting and vetting of agreements.

Model formats of common petitions and applications are available on the Model Drafts page for general reference.
