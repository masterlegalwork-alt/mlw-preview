How a matter moves

The same structured steps for every client. How the chambers work

  1. IntakeBooking, client information form and your documents.
  2. ConsultationIn person, by telephone, on WhatsApp or by video call.
  3. Strategy noteA written note of the options, the forum and the steps ahead.
  4. FilingDrafting, your approval of the draft, and filing.
  5. UpdatesEvery order and next date, with a plain-language note.
  6. ClosureA closing note and the return of your documents and record.
