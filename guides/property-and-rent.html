Guides & FAQs · Property & Rent

Property and rent

Property transfers and tenancies are governed by the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, the Registration Act, 1908 and local tenancy laws. The law on tenancies in Chandigarh changed in 2026.

Property & Rent

Last reviewed 9 October 2026

Stage by stage

  1. Title check

    Before buying, verify the chain of title, revenue records and encumbrances.

    Transfer of Property Act, 1882

  2. Sale deed and registration

    Ownership of immovable property passes by a registered sale deed (s.54 TPA). A GPA, agreement to sell or will is not a transfer of title.

    Suraj Lamp & Industries Pvt. Ltd. v. State of Haryana, (2012) 1 SCC 656 (decided 11.10.2011) · Transfer of Property Act, 1882

  3. Lease and notice

    A lease is defined in s.105 TPA; termination notices are governed by s.106 unless the contract or a local law provides otherwise.

    Transfer of Property Act, 1882

  4. Chandigarh tenancies (2026)

    Reports state that the UT of Chandigarh Tenancy Rules, 2026 operationalise the Assam Tenancy Act, 2021 as extended to Chandigarh, replacing the East Punjab Urban Rent Restriction Act, 1949; the Rent Authority had not been constituted and a challenge was pending at the time of review. Check the current position before acting.

  5. Disputes

    Possession, partition and injunction suits go to civil courts; tenancy disputes go to the forum under the applicable tenancy law.

    Transfer of Property Act, 1882 · Specific Relief Act, 1963

What to prepare

  • Collect the full chain of title documents.
  • Obtain revenue records (jamabandi / fard) where applicable.
  • Keep rent receipts and the tenancy agreement.

Documents to keep ready

  • Sale deed / title documents
  • Revenue records
  • Tenancy agreement and rent receipts
  • Property tax and utility bills

Frequently asked questions

Does a GPA sale give me ownership?

No. The Supreme Court in Suraj Lamp held that transfers by GPA, agreement to sell and will do not convey title; a registered deed of conveyance is needed.

Which tenancy law applies in Chandigarh now?

The position changed in 2026 (see the Tribune report linked in this guide); please check the current status of the rules and any court orders.
