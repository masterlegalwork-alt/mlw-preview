Civil suits and injunctions

A civil suit asks a court to decide rights between private parties: money, property, contracts. An injunction is an order to do or not do something.

Stage by stage

  1. Legal notice

    A notice before suit is often sent; against the Government it is generally mandatory (s.80 CPC).

    Code of Civil Procedure, 1908

  2. Plaint and limitation

    The suit must be filed within the period in the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963. A plaint that discloses no cause of action or is barred by law may be rejected (Order VII Rule 11 CPC).

    Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 · Limitation Act, 1963

  3. Temporary injunction

    The court may grant a temporary injunction during the suit (Order XXXIX Rules 1 and 2 CPC) if there is a prima facie case, the balance of convenience favours it and irreparable injury would otherwise result.

    Dalpat Kumar v. Prahlad Singh, (1992) 1 SCC 719 (decided 16.12.1991) · Code of Civil Procedure, 1908

  4. Written statement and issues

    The defendant files a written statement; the court frames the issues in dispute.

    Salem Advocate Bar Association v. Union of India, (2005) 6 SCC 344 (decided 02.08.2005) · Code of Civil Procedure, 1908

  5. Evidence and arguments

    Evidence is led, usually with examination-in-chief by affidavit, followed by cross-examination and final arguments.

    Code of Civil Procedure, 1908

  6. Decree and execution

    After judgment a decree is drawn up and can be executed under Order XXI CPC.

    Code of Civil Procedure, 1908

What to prepare

  • Collect every document about the transaction in date order.
  • Note the date of the cause of action, which decides limitation.
  • Identify witnesses and their contact details.
  • Keep originals safe; give the advocate copies.

Documents to keep ready

  • Agreements and receipts
  • Title documents (for property)
  • Correspondence and notices
  • Photographs / site plan
  • Earlier court orders

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to file a suit?

It depends on the type of suit; the periods are in the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963 (commonly three years for money claims). Check early.

When will a temporary injunction be granted?

The court looks at a prima facie case, balance of convenience and irreparable injury (Dalpat Kumar v. Prahlad Singh).

What is a permanent injunction?

A final order in the decree restraining a party, governed by ss.36 to 42 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963.
