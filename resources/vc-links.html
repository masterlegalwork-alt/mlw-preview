Court resources

Video-conference links

Official links to join hearings by video conference: the District Courts, Chandigarh, the other District Courts, and the Punjab & Haryana High Court.

Last updated 9 October 2026. Links as published by the courts. Courts change their links and postings; always check the official document on the day of hearing. Inform the court reader of your wish to join by video conference as the court requires. Only official, public links are shown.

Punjab & Haryana High Court

How to join

  1. Find your court room number in the High Court’s cause list.
  2. Request a VC link through the High Court’s Online Video Conference Module after the cause list is published, by 9:00 AM on the date of listing.
  3. The High Court’s list of Zoom meeting IDs for its hybrid VC system (2026) gives each court room a link of the form zoom.us/my/vcphhcNN, where NN is the court room number. Confirm the room on the official list.

P&H High Court website → Video Conferencing → VC Hearing

Rules

VC hearings are governed by the High Court’s video-conferencing rules (2026 rules notified by the High Court). Join on time, keep the camera on, and follow the court’s instructions on naming and conduct.

Source: P&H High Court website (Online Video Conference Module), checked 9 October 2026.
