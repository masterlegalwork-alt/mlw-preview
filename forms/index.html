Free legal formats

Free legal formats

Ten model formats in Word and PDF for common applications and notices before the courts in Chandigarh, Punjab and Haryana.

Representative imagery

Model format for general guidance; adapt to your facts and court; not legal advice. Check the current rules of the court, the stamp and court-fee requirements, and the latest law before use.

Civil Civil

Adjournment application (civil / criminal)

Seeks a short adjournment for a stated, genuine reason. Civil adjournments are limited (proviso to Order XVII Rule 1).

Law: Order XVII Rule 1 CPC, 1908 (civil); Section 346 BNSS, 2023 (Section 309 CrPC, 1973) (criminal)

Word (DOCX)PDF

Application for certified copies

Requests certified copies of orders, judgments or documents on the court file, with the list of pages required.

Law: Rules of the court governing copies; for the accused, Section 404 BNSS, 2023 (Section 363 CrPC, 1973) on copies of judgment

Word (DOCX)PDF

Vakalatnama and memo of appearance

Authorises an advocate to appear and act; a memo of appearance format for counsel is included.

Law: Order III Rule 4 CPC, 1908; Advocates Act, 1961; court rules on vakalatnama and welfare stamps

Word (DOCX)PDF

General affidavit

A general affidavit with numbered paragraphs and a verification clause, for attestation.

Law: Order XIX CPC, 1908; Oaths Act, 1969 (attestation before an Oath Commissioner / Notary)

Word (DOCX)PDF

Application for early hearing

Requests that a matter be taken up earlier than the date fixed, explaining the genuine urgency.

Law: Section 151 CPC, 1908 (civil). In criminal matters before the High Court, the inherent powers under Section 528 BNSS, 2023 (Section 482 CrPC, 1973) may be invoked; follow the court's rules on urgent mentioning

Word (DOCX)PDF

Application for inspection of the court file

Seeks permission for the party or counsel to inspect the judicial file on a stated date.

Law: Section 151 CPC, 1908 and the rules of the court on inspection of records (inspection fee as prescribed)

Word (DOCX)PDF

Application for bringing documents on record

Seeks leave to place additional documents on the record, with a list and the reason they were not filed earlier.

Law: Order VII Rule 14(3), Order VIII Rule 1A(3) and Order XIII Rule 1, read with Section 151 CPC, 1908; for electronic records, Section 63 BSA, 2023 (Section 65B Indian Evidence Act, 1872)

Word (DOCX)PDF

Criminal & Bail Criminal & Bail

Exemption from personal appearance (criminal)

Asks the court to excuse the accused's personal presence on a date, with representation through counsel.

Law: Section 355 BNSS, 2023 (Section 317 CrPC, 1973); for summons cases also Section 228 BNSS (Section 205 CrPC)

Word (DOCX)PDF

Bail application (skeleton)

A skeleton for a regular bail application with headings for FIR details, custody, grounds and undertakings.

Law: Section 480 BNSS, 2023 (Section 437 CrPC) before a Magistrate; Section 483 BNSS (Section 439 CrPC) before the Court of Session / High Court; anticipatory bail Section 482 BNSS (Section 438 CrPC)

Word (DOCX)PDF

Legal notice: cheque dishonour (Section 138 NI Act)

Demand notice to the drawer after a cheque is returned unpaid. It must be sent within 30 days of receiving information of the dishonour; the drawer has 15 days from receipt to pay.

Law: Section 138(b) and (c), Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881
