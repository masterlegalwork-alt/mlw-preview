How the chamber works

From first contact to every hearing

Associates, clerks and counsel coordinate research, drafting and hearing preparation together, so that each matter is prepared with care before it reaches the court.

  1. First contact

    Book online, or call or WhatsApp to arrange a time. A short description of the matter is enough at this stage.

  2. Meeting and papers

    The matter is discussed and you are told which documents are needed.

  3. Fee in writing

    Any fee, and what it covers, is confirmed to you in writing before you are asked to pay.

  4. Preparation and updates

    Research, drafting and hearing preparation are carried out within the chamber; hearing dates go on your Google Calendar.

Our approach

How consultations are handled

A factual outline of how the chamber works with people who consult it.

  • 01

    Document review before the consultation

    Documents sent after booking are studied before the meeting, so the consultation time is used on the questions that matter.

  • 02

    Written note on request

    A short written note of the advice can be prepared after the consultation, on request.

  • 03

    Priority urgent slots

    One slot each day is kept for urgent matters, from 1:30 PM, on special request.

  • 04

    Four consultation modes

    In-person meeting, telephonic call, WhatsApp (voice or written) and video call on Google Meet or WhatsApp video. The fee is the same for every mode.

  • 05

    Three office locations

    Chamber No. 422, District and Sessions Court, Sector 43, Chandigarh; Room No. 1, New Bar Complex, Punjab & Haryana High Court, Chandigarh; and Corner Kothi No. 223, Phase 2, Sector 54, SAS Nagar (Mohali).

What every client receives

How the chamber keeps clients informed.

A private case folder for every client

Each client gets a private Google Drive folder for the matter: documents, drafts and court orders in one place, reachable in at most three clicks.
