Chambers

The chambers

Master Legal Work is the chamber of Advocate Gagandeep Goel at Chamber No. 422, District and Sessions Court, Sector 43, Chandigarh.

How the chamber works

Associates, clerks and counsel coordinate research, drafting and hearing preparation together, so that each matter is prepared with care before it reaches the court.

  1. First contact

    Call, WhatsApp or email to arrange a time. A short description of the matter is enough at this stage.

  2. Meeting and papers

    The matter is discussed and you are told which documents are needed.

  3. Fee in writing

    Any fee, and what it covers, is confirmed to you in writing before you are asked to pay.

  4. Preparation and updates

    Research, drafting and hearing preparation are carried out within the chamber, and you are kept informed of hearing dates.

Our principles

Service, excellence, integrity, justice.

These four words are the chamber’s brand line. They describe how the chamber intends to work; they are not a promise about the outcome of any case.

Visiting the chamber

The chamber is on the court premises, at Chamber No. 422 of the District and Sessions Court, Sector 43, Chandigarh. Please call or send a WhatsApp message before visiting.
