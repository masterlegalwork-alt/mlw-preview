Chambers

The chambers

How the chamber works

Associates, clerks and counsel coordinate research, drafting and hearing preparation together for each matter before it reaches the court.

  1. Initial communication

    You write, call or book online. A short description of the matter is enough at this stage.

  2. Preliminary conflict and matter assessment

    The chambers check that they can act for you and whether the matter is one they can consider, and tell you before any payment.

  3. Consultation and consideration of documents

    The documents you share are read and the matter is discussed at the confirmed time.

  4. Further steps, where separately agreed

    Any further work, and its fee, is agreed with you separately in writing.

Our approach

A factual outline of how the chamber works with people who consult it.

  • 01

    Document review before the consultation

    Documents sent after booking are studied before the meeting, so the consultation time is used on the questions that matter.

  • 02

    Written note on request

    A short written note of the advice can be prepared after the consultation, on request.

  • 03

    Priority urgent slots

    One slot each day is kept for urgent matters, from 1:30 PM, on special request.

  • 04

    Four consultation modes

    In-person meeting, telephonic call, WhatsApp (voice or written) and video call on Google Meet or WhatsApp video. The fee is the same for every mode.

  • 05

    Three office locations

    Chamber No. 422, 4th Floor, Distt. & Sessions Court, Sector 43, Chandigarh 160043; Room No. 1, New Bar Complex, Punjab & Haryana High Court, Chandigarh; and Corner Kothi No. 223, Phase 2, Sector 54, SAS Nagar (Mohali).

How consultations are arranged

How the chambers keep clients informed.

  • I

    Updates on every matter, free of charge

    Basic updates on your case, such as the next date and a copy of each order, are always provided free of charge.

  • II

    Optional: the MLW Client Portal

    For organised digital access: a private Google Drive case folder, order sheets shared within 24 hours of availability, and hearing dates and reminders on your own Google Calendar. An optional premium service.

  • III

    Documents studied before you meet

    Papers you share after booking are reviewed before the consultation, so the time is spent on the questions that matter.

  • IV

    Free legal updates and resources

    Legal updates, guides, FAQs, free formats and court resources, free to use without signing up.

  • V

    Four modes of consultation

    In person, by telephone, on WhatsApp or by video call. One slot each day is kept for urgent matters, from 1:30 PM, on special request.

  • VI

    Three office locations

    Chamber No. 422, 4th Floor, Distt. & Sessions Court, Sector 43, Chandigarh 160043; Room No. 1, New Bar Complex at the High Court; and a home office in Sector 54, Mohali (by appointment only).
