Consultation

Book a consultation

Consultations with Advocate Gagandeep Goel are by appointment and are prepaid. Choose a mode below. Every mode is ₹5,500 for each 30 minutes; an urgent consultation, on a priority basis, is double: ₹11,000 for each 30 minutes. A consultation goes ahead only after the payment is received.

✦ Early-booking benefit — for consultations booked seven or more days in advance, you may choose either 10% off the fee or a complimentary extra 15 minutes. Not applicable to urgent consultations.

Recommendation: Legal consultations generally take time. To allow a full understanding of the subject matter and better-considered advice, we recommend booking a slot of at least 1 hour.

✦ What do you need?

Every service is prepaid and uses this same form. For services marked [fee to be confirmed], the fee and turnaround are confirmed to you in writing before any payment. Choose a mode below for how we should contact you.

Before you book

Legal consultations generally take time. To allow a full understanding of the subject matter and better-considered advice, we recommend booking a slot of at least 1 hour.

Timely, well-informed legal advice at the outset can help you understand your options, avoid unnecessary or prolonged litigation, and reduce avoidable costs.

A 30-minute slot remains available for shorter queries.

Sharing your documents securely

This website does not take case-document uploads. After booking, please share your documents through Google Drive:

  1. Open Google Drive and create a folder named [Your Name - Booking Ref], for example “Ravi Kumar - MLW-261009-AB12”.
  2. Upload your case documents (orders, pleadings, FIR, notices, agreements, receipts) into it, preferably as clear PDFs.
  3. Click Share, add masterlegalwork@gmail.com and give it Editor access.
  4. Email or WhatsApp us the folder link, quoting your booking reference.

Smaller sets can be sent directly to masterlegalwork@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 9872206969.
