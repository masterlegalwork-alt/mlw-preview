Consultation

Book a consultation

Consultations with Advocate Gagandeep Goel are by appointment and are prepaid. Choose a mode below. Every mode is ₹5,500 for each 30 minutes; an urgent consultation, on a priority basis, is double: ₹11,000 for each 30 minutes. A consultation goes ahead only after the payment is received.

Recommendation: Legal consultations generally take time. To allow a full understanding of the subject matter and better-considered advice, we recommend booking a slot of at least 1 hour.

1 Choose the mode of consultation

Before you book

Legal consultations generally take time. To allow a full understanding of the subject matter and better-considered advice, we recommend booking a slot of at least 1 hour.

Timely, well-informed legal advice at the outset can help you understand your options, avoid unnecessary or prolonged litigation, and reduce avoidable costs.

A 30-minute slot remains available for shorter queries.
