How consultations are arranged

Practical arrangements that keep you informed at every stage. A description of how the chambers work, not a promise about any outcome.

  • I

    Updates on every matter, free of charge

    Basic updates on your case, such as the next date and a copy of each order, are always provided free of charge.

  • II

    Optional: the MLW Client Portal

    For organised digital access: a private Google Drive case folder, order sheets shared within 24 hours of availability, and hearing dates and reminders on your own Google Calendar. An optional premium service.

  • III

    Documents studied before you meet

    Papers you share after booking are reviewed before the consultation, so the time is spent on the questions that matter.

  • IV

    Free legal updates and resources

    Legal updates, guides, FAQs, free formats and court resources, free to use without signing up.

  • V

    Four modes of consultation

    In person, by telephone, on WhatsApp or by video call. One slot each day is kept for urgent matters, from 1:30 PM, on special request.

  • VI

    Three office locations

    Chamber No. 422 at the District Courts, Sector 43; Room No. 1, New Bar Complex at the High Court; and a home office in Sector 54, Mohali (by appointment only).

How your matter is managed

From the first booking to every hearing date, you can see where your matter stands.

  1. Book and share your documents

    Book a consultation online, then share your papers through a Google Drive folder named [Your Name - Booking Ref], shared with masterlegalwork@gmail.com.

    Before the meeting

  2. Consultation, and updates on your matter

    After the consultation you receive the next date and a copy of each order, free of charge, on WhatsApp or email.

    Always free

  3. Optional: the MLW Client Portal

    With the Client Portal, a private Google Drive case folder is shared with you, and hearing dates and reminders are added to your own Google Calendar, for the life of the matter.

Areas of practice

General information only, not an offer of services. All areas of practice
