The Master Legal Work difference

What every client receives

Practical arrangements that keep you informed at every stage of your matter. A description of how the chamber works, not a promise about any outcome.

A private case folder for every client

Each client gets a private Google Drive folder for the matter: documents, drafts and court orders in one place, reachable in at most three clicks.

Hearing dates on your own calendar

Hearing dates are added to your Google Calendar with reminders, so the next date is always in front of you.

Documents studied before you meet

Papers you share after booking are reviewed before the consultation, so the time is spent on the questions that matter.

Free legal updates and resources

Daily legal updates, guides, FAQs, free formats and court resources, free to use without signing up.

Four modes, priority urgent slots

In person, telephone, WhatsApp or video call at the same fee, with one priority slot each day for urgent matters from 1:30 PM.

Three office locations

Chamber No. 422 at the District Courts, Sector 43; Room No. 1, New Bar Complex at the High Court; and Sector 54, Mohali.
